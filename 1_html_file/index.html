Goodstev Integrated Services Ltd

Integrated Engineering
& Industrial Solutions

Goodstev Integrated Services Ltd (RC 7033437) is an active, NUPRC-licensed oil and gas service company incorporated on 30 June 2023 under CAMA 2020. Operating from Port Harcourt, Rivers State, we deliver engineering, supply, pipeline, mechanical, and electrical services to upstream and downstream operators across the Niger Delta.

  • Indigenous Port Harcourt firm with direct Niger Delta operational experience
  • Corporate QA/QC manual and HSE plan governing every project we execute
  • Proven track record delivering for operators across the Niger Delta since 2023
