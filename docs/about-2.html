Engineering Solutions

GOODSTEV INTEGRATED SERVICES LTD is a Nigerian indigenous company providing engineering, construction, Oil & Gas and procurement solutions to clients across diverse project environments. We combine technical expertise, practical experience and a commitment to quality to deliver solutions that meet client requirements and industry standards.

  • Technical expertise across Oil & Gas, engineering and construction
  • Commitment to quality, safety and client satisfaction
  • Practical solutions tailored to client requirements

Quality &
Reliability

We maintain a strong commitment to quality across engineering, procurement, construction and field operations, with services delivered in line with client requirements, applicable standards and established quality procedures.

Technical Expertise

Our capabilities span Oil & Gas field engineering, instrumentation, mechanical and electrical engineering, civil construction, pipeline services and project support, allowing us to provide practical solutions across different project requirements.
